The structure, content, and length of the dissertation proposal are set by the dissertation director.
The student should meet with their director soon after passing qualifying exams.
In that meeting, the director will specify their expectations for the dissertation proposal.

The following guidelines are advisory, not binding.
They are offered as a suggestion and point of departure.

# Title and abstract

The title should be descriptive, modeled on recent book titles in your field of study.

The abstract is a non-technical summary of the proposed dissertation project, comprehensible to educated non-specialists, describing both the nature of the project and its significance for the field.
The abstract should be no more than 300 words.

# Technical description

Describe in appropriate detail the proposed dissertation project:

- **Research question**.
  State the research question to be investigated and tentative direction of your argument.
- **Scope**.
  Justify the contours of the project, including period, region, genre, authors.
- **Previous work and significance**.
  Explain how the project is linked to previous scholarship in its field of study and what the project is expected to contribute.
  What is the current state of study in the proposed research area?
  What aspects of the proposed dissertation are innovative?
  How will the project advance the state of knowledge in its field?
- **Methods, theory, and evidence**.
  Identify the research methods or theoretical approaches to be used, and the primary forms of evidence.
  Briefly explain their relevance to the research question.
- **Chapter outline**.
  Provide tentative chapter titles and brief sketches for the content of each chapter. 
- **Works cited**.
  Supply citations and a bibliography in a format appropriate to the field of study.

This section should be no more than 2,000 words, excluding works cited.

# Extramural resources

If resources external to Loyola are required (e.g., access to archival documents held by other institutions, acceptances in programs offered at other institutions, additional funding by outside agencies), specify those resources and indicate what preparation you have made to secure them.
Also indicate what alternative means will be employed to complete your project if outside resources are not forthcoming.
This section should be no more than 300 words.
It may be omitted if there are no required external resources.

# Timeline and dissemination plan

Delineate a timeline for completion of the project.
Also identify your plans for developing portions of this project for article submission or presentation at conferences.
This section should be no more than 300 words.
